The situation

Describe the client's context — industry, scale, what they were trying to solve, and why they came to you. Anonymise as needed but keep it concrete enough to be credible.

What was the specific problem? What had they already tried? Why wasn't it working?

The approach

Walk through what you actually did. Be specific about the methodology — vague claims don't build trust. If you can't share proprietary details, explain the shape of the work without the secrets.

  • First major workstream or phase
  • Second workstream with brief description
  • Third workstream or decision point that mattered

What moved the needle

Not everything you tried worked — that's fine. Be honest about what the key lever was and why. The insight is usually more interesting than the execution.

The single most important change was X, because it addressed Y that everyone else was ignoring.

The results

Numbers with context. Don't just list metrics — explain what they mean and why they're the right thing to measure. Acknowledge any caveats in the data.
